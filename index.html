    About NoteCred

    NoteCred is a Student Academic Resource & Project Sharing Platform designed as one organized place for subject-wise notes, seniors' guidance, and past student projects — a knowledge base that grows with every batch.

    Core Objectives

    • Central platform to upload, search, and share academic resources.
    • Let students request notes directly from seniors and peers.
    • Maintain a repository of previous projects with improvement ideas.
    • Provide ratings, usefulness votes, and subject categories.
    • Deliver a clean, responsive experience across all devices.
